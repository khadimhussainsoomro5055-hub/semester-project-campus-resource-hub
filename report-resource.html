What Can You Report?

You can report the following types of content:

  • Incorrect or outdated study material
  • Duplicate uploads
  • Irrelevant or inappropriate files
  • Misleading physical book listings

What Happens Next?

Once submitted, your report will be sent to the administrator for review. The admin can investigate and resolve the issue.
