Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using Campus Resource Hub.

1.   How can I upload a resource?
Go to the "Upload Resource" page, select the category, add a title and description, choose your file, and submit. The resource will be reviewed by the admin before it becomes visible to other students.
2.   How long does it take for my uploaded resource to be approved?
Your uploaded resource will remain pending until an administrator reviews it. You can check its approval status from the My Uploads page.
3.   What types of files are allowed?
You can upload supported academic files such as PDF, DOC, DOCX, PPT, PPTX, XLS, XLSX, ZIP, JPG, JPEG, and PNG files, subject to the platform's upload size limit.
4.   Can I request a physical book from another student?
Yes. Open the Physical Books page, find the book you need, and select Request to Borrow. The owner can approve or reject your request.
5.   How can I track the status of my requests?
Visit the My Requests page to see whether your borrowing request is pending, approved, rejected, or completed.
6.   What should I do if I find inappropriate content?
Use the Report Content option to report inappropriate, irrelevant, duplicate, or incorrect resources. The administrator will review your report.
7.   Can I edit or delete my uploaded resource?
You can manage your uploaded resources from the My Uploads page. Available editing and deletion options depend on the resource's status and platform permissions.
8.   Who can see my email address?
Your university email is used for your account. You can review your profile visibility and email privacy preferences from the Settings page.
